# moai-reshaped — A6 check of everything on screen (VO Kokoro, 55.0 s; opens on a platform statue)

Each on-screen string or picture, and the SOURCES item in `script/moai-reshaped.txt` it rests on. "LH25" is Lipo & Hunt 2025, J. Archaeol. Sci. 183, 106383. A7: `factcheck/moai-reshaped.json` (12/12 confirmed) covers the spoken script only; this file covers the pictures and the overlays and needs its own A7 pass before approval.

Built 2026-11-24 as one 3D world (`tools/blender/moai_world.py` → `public/3d/moai-world.mp4`, with `public/3d/moai-world-b.mp4` for half-second cross-dissolves at each cut, as in cleopatra-pyramid). Labels, width bars, lean lines and counters are Remotion overlays pinned to the statues through `src/generated/moai-world.anchors.json`. Shapes are procedural; surfaces are CC0 Poly Haven scans, credited in `public/SOURCES.json`.

**The statues** are generic moai lofted from a profile (long head, heavy brow, long nose, pouting lips, long ears, hands on the belly), not a portrait of any statue (C6). No statue is shown broken or damaged. Proportions follow LH25 only qualitatively: road statues have a base about as wide as the shoulders (drawn 0.99 of the shoulder width) and lean forward (drawn 9°, inside LH25's 6–15°); platform statues stand upright with a narrower base (drawn 0.84 of the shoulder width) and the front of the base cut back. **These are not the corrected Zenodo means** (SOURCES 7), which A7 could not open; no ratio or angle is written on screen, and every shot with a width bar says "proportions not to a measured ratio". The road statue 12-220-01 (7.35 m tall, 2.83 m wide; LH25 1.4) sets the general shape.

**Gold ghosts** are never stone: a gold outline, see-through, used three times, and each time the small print says what it is. (1) The hook: the road shape over a platform statue, "As it arrived? · their reading". (2) The trimming: the road shape left standing while the statue inside it loses its lean and the front of its base. (3) The close: upright statues where fallen ones lie, "Another reading".

**The ancient walk** shows ropes only, running out of frame, no people: nothing read says who pulled or how many. **The test** shows people because LH25 1.4 says how many and where: "four on each lateral rope and ten on the rear rope". They are plain mannequins in modern work clothes, with no faces and a range of skin tones; nobody is a portrait (C4). The test site is a plain field: the place is not shown.

**Not sourced, flag for A7:**
- The platform (ahu): a stone platform on the coast with the statues facing inland, the sea behind them. This is the common arrangement, but nothing in SOURCES says it, and the stonework is generic.
- The replica's size: LH25 gives 4.35 t but no size in the text read. It is drawn 2.96 m tall, scaled from the statue shape at an assumed 2.3 t/m³ for concrete. The small print says "replica's size estimated from its weight". No size is written on screen.
- Rano Raraku as a steep volcanic cone with a crater and the quarry face on its south-west flank: drawn from general knowledge, not from SOURCES. The island is compressed and the roads and statues are spaced for legibility ("island not to scale").
- The roads as faint reddish earth tracks about 6 m wide: no width is sourced.
