## age-of-sunlight-long

```
Sunlight is old news. Neutrinos aren't ☀️
Neutrinos from fusion in the Sun's core reach you about 8 minutes after they're made. The energy that becomes sunlight takes tens of thousands of years just to get out. In 2014 the Borexino detector's neutrino count matched what sunlight predicts to within about 10%.
Sources: Borexino, Nature 2014; Mitalas & Sills 1992 (ApJ); NASA.
Synthetic narration. Images: NASA SDO, STEREO, ISS.
#sun #physics #neutrinos #space #astronomy
```

## great-red-spot

```
Was the Great Red Spot really seen in 1665? 🪐
Cassini saw a dark oval there in 1665. After 1713 it vanishes from every surviving observation for 118 years. The storm we call the Great Red Spot first shows up in drawings in 1831, and a 2024 study says Cassini's spot was most likely a different storm.
Source: Sánchez-Lavega et al. 2024, Geophysical Research Letters.
Illustration on NASA/JPL/SSI's Cassini map of Jupiter, with the Great Red Spot removed for the scenes before 1831. Synthetic narration.
#jupiter #greatredspot #astronomy #space
```
