The sunlight on your skin is a hundred thousand years old.
Eight minutes of that was the trip from the Sun.
The rest was getting out.
But it is not the same light.
No photon survives the climb.
The energy is ancient. The light is eight minutes old.

SOURCES (for approval — every claim in the script, with the number behind it)

1. "Eight minutes of that was the trip from the Sun."
   1 AU = 149,597,870.7 km; c = 299,792.458 km/s, so 499.0 s = 8 min 19 s from the
   photosphere to Earth. Uncontested. "From the Sun" means its visible surface,
   which is where the light we actually see leaves.

2. "A hundred thousand years old."
   The time energy takes to cross the radiative zone, from the fusing core to the
   base of the convective zone. This is the softest number in the script, and the
   published figures disagree by more than an order of magnitude. Mitalas & Sills
   (1992), "On the photon diffusion time scale for the Sun", ApJ 401, 759, compute
   ~1.7e5 years; other treatments give ~1e4 to ~1e5; and the naive random walk with
   a millimetre mean free path over-estimates into the millions because it ignores
   how the opacity falls off outward. "A hundred thousand" is a round figure inside
   the accepted range, not a measured value.
   Tighter alternatives if one is wanted: "tens of thousands of years" is safe and
   uncontested, and "about a hundred and seventy thousand years" is the usual
   citable figure. Confirm the Mitalas & Sills volume and page against the journal
   before this goes out — it is quoted here from memory, not from the paper.

3. "The rest was getting out."
   Core out to ~0.7 solar radii is the radiative zone, where the energy diffuses
   and claim 2's time is spent. From 0.7 R to the surface is the convective zone,
   which carries energy by bulk motion of plasma in weeks to months — negligible
   against the radiative-zone figure. That is why the short animates a long grind
   and then a fast break-out, rather than one uniform crawl.

4. "But it is not the same light. No photon survives the climb."
   Fusion in the core emits gamma rays. Those are absorbed and re-emitted by the
   plasma countless times on the way out, each interaction degrading the energy,
   and what leaves the photosphere is a ~5,772 K blackbody spectrum: mostly visible
   light. One core gamma photon's energy ends up spread over of order a million
   visible photons (~1 MeV divided by ~2 eV). So no individual photon makes the
   trip, which is exactly the part the popular version of this fact drops. It is
   in the script because without it the hook is false.

5. "The energy is ancient. The light is eight minutes old."
   Rests on claims 1, 2 and 4. No new number.

6. Zone fractions used by the animation, not spoken.
   Fusion is essentially all inside 0.25 R; the radiative/convective boundary is at
   ~0.7 R; core ~15.7 million K against a ~5,772 K photosphere. Standard solar
   model figures, uncontested at this precision.
