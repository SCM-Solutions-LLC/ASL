The fourth wife beat them all.

Henry VIII had six wives, and the one he couldn't wait to get rid of outlived
every single one.

Wife one, Catherine of Aragon. 24 years of marriage, annulled. Henry broke
England away from Rome to do it.

Wife two, Anne Boleyn. She gave him a daughter, the future Elizabeth I. Three
years in, Henry had her executed.

Wife three, Jane Seymour. She finally gave him a son, then died 12 days later.

Wife four, Anne of Cleves. Henry met her once and wanted out. Six months later,
annulled, but she walked away with a palace, a castle, and a new title: the
King's beloved sister.

Wife five, Catherine Howard. Executed.

Wife six, Catherine Parr. She outlived Henry.

Divorced, beheaded, died. Divorced, beheaded, survived. But here's what the
rhyme leaves out. Anne of Cleves outlived Henry and all five other wives. The
wife he rejected was the last one standing.
