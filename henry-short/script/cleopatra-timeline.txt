SOURCES (for approval — every claim in the script, with the number behind it)

Checked 9 Nov 2026: c. 2560 BC and Khufu's c. 2589-2566 BC are the conventional
dates in general reference works (World History Encyclopedia, New World
Encyclopedia); the arithmetic in 3, 4, 5 and 7 was recomputed and is right.
Cleopatra's dates and Apollo 11's were not re-fetched; they are not in doubt.

1. "The pyramid was finished around twenty-five sixty BC."
   The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for Khufu, whose reign is conventionally
   given as c. 2589-2566 BC; c. 2560 BC is the usual round figure for its
   completion. This is the softest number in the script: Old Kingdom chronology
   carries an uncertainty of several decades, and published dates for Khufu
   differ by that much between chronologies. "Around" is in the line for that
   reason. The uncertainty is tens of years against a margin of about 450, so
   it cannot change the comparison.

2. "Cleopatra was born in sixty-nine BC."
   Cleopatra VII Philopator: born early 69 BC (some reconstructions put it in
   late 70), died August 30 BC. Uncontested at this precision. She was the last
   ruler of the Ptolemaic dynasty and Macedonian Greek by descent, which is why
   the title card says "Queen of Egypt" rather than "Egyptian queen".

3. "Two and a half thousand years later."
   2560 BC to 69 BC is 2,491 years. Rounded up in the read; the on-screen count
   is the unrounded 2,491.

4. "From her to Apollo eleven is only two thousand."
   Apollo 11 landed on 20 July 1969. From 69 BC to AD 1969 is 2,037 years —
   68 + 1,969, because there is no year zero. The on-screen count is 2,037.
   Measured from her death in 30 BC instead, it is 1,998.

5. "Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the
   Great Pyramid."
   Rests on 1-4: 2,491 years back against 2,037 forward from her birth, a
   difference of 454, which is the figure on the closing label. From her death
   the gap is wider still (2,530 against 1,998). The claim holds on either
   reckoning and for any defensible date for the pyramid.

6. "To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient history."
   Follows from 3. No new number. The pyramids were standing, famous and
   visited in her time — "ancient history", not ruins.

7. Scale used by the animation, not spoken.
   The three markers are placed along the line in proportion to the years
   (astronomical year numbering: -2559, -68, 1969), so Cleopatra stands 55.0%
   of the way from the pyramid to the Moon. The positions are computed from the
   dates in the code, not placed by eye.
