Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
The pyramid was finished around twenty-six hundred BC.
Cleopatra was born in sixty-nine BC. Two and a half thousand years later.
From her to Apollo eleven is only two thousand.
To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient history.

1. "The pyramid was finished around twenty-six hundred BC."
   The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for Khufu, during his reign: Sheisha
   2022 calls it "the Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu Pyramid)", and the Journal
   of Merer records limestone taken "to the construction site of the Great
   Pyramid of Khufu". No source states a completion year, so
   the line dates it by the end of his reign: median 2565-2603 BC in Erdil
   2025's models, c. 2600 BC as a round figure. Published dates for Khufu's
   accession differ by more than a century (2613-2577 radiocarbon down to
   2480 +/- 5 Spence, in Sheisha 2022); on Spence's low dates (Khafre's
   accession 2448) the pyramid would be about 150 years younger. "Around" is in
   the line, and every on-screen date carries "c.". Even on the lowest
   chronology the pyramid is about 2,380 years older than Cleopatra, against
   2,037 years from her to Apollo 11, so the comparison holds.

2. "Cleopatra was born in sixty-nine BC."
   Cleopatra VII: born 69 BC, died August 30 BC, "the last member of Ptolemaic
   Dynasty" and "the last queen of Egypt" (Rosso 2021), which is why the title
   card says "Queen of Egypt".

3. "Two and a half thousand years later."
   2600 BC to 69 BC is 2,531 years; on Erdil's medians 2,496-2,534. The
   on-screen count shows 2,531 marked "YEARS, ROUGHLY", because it inherits the
   c. of the pyramid date.

4. "From her to Apollo eleven is only two thousand."
   Apollo 11 landed on 20 July 1969 (NASA image records). From 69 BC to AD 1969 is 2,037 years —
   68 + 1,969, because there is no year zero. The on-screen count is 2,037.
   Measured from her death in 30 BC instead, it is 1,998.

5. "Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the
   Great Pyramid."
   Rests on 1-4: 2,531 years back against 2,037 forward from her birth, a
   difference of 494 (459-497 on Erdil's medians), shown on the
   closing label as "about 500". From her death the gap is wider still (2,570 against 1,998). The claim holds on either
   reckoning and for any defensible date for the pyramid.

6. "To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient history."
   Follows from 3. In context, "the pyramids" are those at Giza: the youngest,
   Menkaure's, was begun after his accession, 2564-2524 BC (radiocarbon) or
   2532 BC (Shaw), both in Sheisha 2022, so the three were built roughly 2,300
   to 2,500 years before her birth, depending on the chronology. Later
   pyramids, such as the Nubian ones at Meroe, are not the subject; the title
   says "the Great Pyramid" for that reason.

7. Scale used by the animation, not spoken.
   The three markers are placed along the line in proportion to the years
   (astronomical year numbering: -2599, -68, 1969), so Cleopatra stands 55.4%
   of the way from the pyramid to the Moon. The positions are computed from the
   dates in the code, not placed by eye.
