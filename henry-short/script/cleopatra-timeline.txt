Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
The pyramid was finished around twenty-five sixty BC.
Cleopatra was born in sixty-nine BC. Two and a half thousand years later.
From her to Apollo eleven is only two thousand.
To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient history.

- Erdil et al., PLOS ONE (2025), doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0314612: the High and

- Roller, Cleopatra: A Biography (Oxford University Press, 2010), ISBN

1. "The pyramid was finished around twenty-five sixty BC."
   The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for Khufu (Sheisha 2022: "the Great
   Pyramid of Giza (Khufu Pyramid)"); c. 2560 BC is the usual round figure for its
   completion, between the end of Khufu's reign and Khafre's accession on the
   radiocarbon and Shaw dates (Sheisha 2022). No source states a completion
   year, and published dates for Khufu differ by up to about a century: the
   lowest (Spence, 2480 BC) would make the pyramid about 80 years younger. c.
   2560 is used because the radiocarbon model supports the higher chronology.
   "Around" is in the line, and every on-screen date carries "c.". Even on the
   lowest chronology the pyramid is about 2,400 years older than Cleopatra,
   against 2,037 years from her to Apollo 11, so the comparison holds.

2. "Cleopatra was born in sixty-nine BC."
   Cleopatra VII Philopator: born around the beginning of 69 BC (Roller; Plutarch
   allows late 70), died August 30 BC (Roller). She was the last
   ruler of the Ptolemaic dynasty and Macedonian Greek by descent, which is why
   the title card says "Queen of Egypt" rather than "Egyptian queen".

3. "Two and a half thousand years later."
   2560 BC to 69 BC is 2,491 years; on the radiocarbon brackets 2,480-2,520.
   The on-screen count shows 2,491 marked "YEARS, ROUGHLY", because it inherits
   the c. of the pyramid date.

4. "From her to Apollo eleven is only two thousand."
   Apollo 11 landed on 20 July 1969 (NASA). From 69 BC to AD 1969 is 2,037 years —
   68 + 1,969, because there is no year zero. The on-screen count is 2,037.
   Measured from her death in 30 BC instead, it is 1,998.

5. "Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the
   Great Pyramid."
   Rests on 1-4: 2,491 years back against 2,037 forward from her birth, a
   difference of 454 (440-480 on the radiocarbon brackets), shown on the
   closing label as "about 450". From her death
   the gap is wider still (2,530 against 1,998). The claim holds on either
   reckoning and for any defensible date for the pyramid.

6. "To Cleopatra, the pyramids were already ancient history."
   Follows from 3. In context, "the pyramids" are those at Giza: the youngest,
   Menkaure's, has an accession date of 2564-2524 BC (radiocarbon) or 2532 BC
   (Shaw), both in Sheisha 2022, so all three were some 2,450 years old or more
   at her birth. Diodorus Siculus (1.63), writing in her century, already
   treated the largest as more than a thousand years old. Later pyramids, such
   as the Nubian ones at Meroe, are not the subject; the title says "the Great
   Pyramid" for that reason.

7. Scale used by the animation, not spoken.
   The three markers are placed along the line in proportion to the years
   (astronomical year numbering: -2559, -68, 1969), so Cleopatra stands 55.0%
   of the way from the pyramid to the Moon. The positions are computed from the
   dates in the code, not placed by eye.
